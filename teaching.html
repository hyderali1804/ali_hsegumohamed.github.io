Teaching

Teaching

Three decades of teaching across Electronics & Communication, Electrical & Electronics, Information Technology and Computer Engineering departments - at Ashesi University (Ghana), Buraydah Colleges (Saudi Arabia), Guilin University of Aerospace Technology (China) and multiple institutions in India.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching is centred on nurturing critical thinking, innovation and problem-solving. I believe in an engaging, inclusive classroom where students are encouraged to ask hard questions, tinker with real systems, and push the boundaries of their knowledge. I lean heavily on demonstrations, project-based learning and reflective assessment, supported by modern LMS workflows.
