Research

Research Programme

My research lies at the intersection of signal & image processing, medical imaging and applied deep learning - with a strong commitment to translating algorithms into tools that improve clinical care.

Research Areas

Medical Image Denoising & Enhancement

Curvelet, wavelet and total-variation thresholding methods for MR and CT image denoising - preserving edges while suppressing noise.

CurveletWaveletMRICT

Deep Learning for Healthcare

Convolutional and transformer architectures for diabetic retinopathy detection, medical image segmentation and computer-aided diagnosis.

CNNRetinopathyCAD

Machine Learning & Cybersecurity

Behavior-enhanced social bot detection and URL-based spammer identification on online social networks.

MLOSNSecurity

IoT, Smart Systems & Sensor Networks

IoT-driven smart irrigation, geometric sampling algorithms for sensor fields, and economics-aware cloud deployment.

IoTSensorsCloud

Current Project

Diabetic Retinopathy Detection using Deep Learning - Building convolutional models for early and accurate detection of diabetic retinopathy from retinal fundus images. The goal is to support clinicians with fast, reliable AI-assisted screening, particularly in resource-constrained settings.

Doctoral Thesis

"Certain Investigations on Denoising Techniques for Brain MR Images Using Curvelet Transform" - Anna University, India, 2011.

Supervised by Prof. Dr. R. Sukanesh, Thiyagarajar College of Engineering, Madurai. The work proposed edge-preserving denoising of brain MRI using digital curvelet transforms and hybrid wavelet + total-variation thresholding.
