About

I hold a PhD in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Anna University, India (2011), with a doctoral thesis on "Certain Investigations on Denoising Techniques for Brain MR Images Using Curvelet Transform." My career has spanned every academic rank - from Lecturer to Professor - across institutions in India, Saudi Arabia, China and Ghana.

I currently serve as Professor of Electrical & Electronics Engineering at Ashesi University, Ghana and have served as a Visiting Faculty at Guilin University of Aerospace Technology, China. My teaching philosophy centres on nurturing critical thinking, innovation and problem-solving - creating an inclusive learning environment where students push the boundaries of their knowledge.

My research philosophy is rooted in impactful and practical solutions to real-world problems. Recent work in deep learning for healthcare - including diabetic retinopathy detection - reflects my commitment to bridging theoretical research and practical implementation. I also contribute as a reviewer for journals including Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing (Springer) and Journal of Super Computing (Springer).
